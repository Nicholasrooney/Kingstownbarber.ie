FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to book, or can I just drop in?

It's best to call ahead on (01) 288 5571 so we can have a slot ready, but if something's urgent ring us — we regularly fit people in at short notice.

Will servicing with you affect my manufacturer warranty?

No. Under EU rules you can have your car serviced by an independent garage without affecting the warranty, as long as the work follows the manufacturer's schedule and uses parts of matching quality — which is exactly what we do. We stamp your service book too.

Can you get my car ready for the NCT?

Yes. We'll do a pre-NCT check, fix anything likely to fail, and handle any repairs on your fail sheet if you need a retest.

Do you work on vans?

Yes — we service and repair vans as well as cars of all makes and models.

Can you check a car before I buy it?

Yes. Give us a call to arrange a pre-purchase inspection so you know exactly what you're buying.

Where can I park when dropping off?

Pull in at the garage at 1 Sweetman's Avenue, Newtown, Blackrock — just off the main street and a short walk from Blackrock DART station.
